=== SLIDE 2 ===
  - SLIDE 2
  - Background / Motivation
  - Context of the Problem
  - Health, education, agriculture & infrastructure data is fragmented and manually collected.
  - Citizens and Sarpanch officials are affected by slow, inconsistent local governance.
  - Why This Project Is Important
  - Panchayats often lack a unified, ward-wise view of a problem's scale and severity.
  - AI/ML enables automatic detection, transparent scoring, and evidence-based scheme matching.
  - Link to SDG Goals
  - SDG 11: strengthens inclusive, data-driven local governance and planning.
  - SDG 16: builds accountable, transparent Panchayat-level institutions.

=== SLIDE 3 ===
  - SLIDE 3
  - Problem Statement
  - Problem Definition
  - Village-level decisions rely on fragmented, manually-collected data, so the Gram Panchayat cannot easily see which wards need action first.
  - Scope
  - Included:  Household/family registry, Sarpanch dashboard, multi-agent domain analysis, priority scoring, RAG scheme matching, multilingual text (EN/HI/MR).
  - Excluded:  Real government-data integration, GIS mapping, offline/mobile deployment (Phase-1 prototype uses synthetic/demo data).
  - Challenges in Current Scenario
  - Manual, paper-based household and issue records are slow and error-prone.
  - No real-time, unified visibility into ward-wise development indicators.
  - Scheme-matching and prioritization are done informally, without documented evidence.
  - SY B.Tech CSE (AIML)  |  Project First Review

=== SLIDE 4 ===
  - SLIDE 4
  - Objectives
  - Specific, Measurable Objectives
  - Build a digital household/village registry with automatic population calculation from member records.
  - Design a 13-agent multi-agent AI architecture for health, education, agriculture, water, infrastructure & finance analysis.
  - Implement transparent, explainable 0–100 priority scoring to rank village problems.
  - Provide multilingual (English/Hindi/Marathi) interaction and RAG-based, source-cited government-scheme matching.
  - Alignment with SDG Goals
  - Objectives 1 & 3 advance SDG 16 (accountable institutions); Objective 4 advances SDG 11 & SDG 10 (inclusive, equitable access).

=== SLIDE 8 ===
  - SLIDE 8
  - Ethical Considerations
  - Possible Unethical Practices
  - Misuse or unauthorized sharing of sensitive household data (income, health, caste).
  - Biased priority scores skewing resources toward already well-served wards.
  - Presenting AI recommendations as if they were official government decisions.
  - Ethical Alternatives
  - Role-based access control, encryption in transit, and audit logging for household records.
  - Priority-score weights validated with faculty/Panchayat context; fairness checks across wards.
  - Recommendations always labeled advisory — final decisions remain with Panchayat officials.
  - Commitment to Professional Ethics
  - We commit to using synthetic/demo data during prototyping, citing all scheme sources, and following the institute's Code of Conduct.
